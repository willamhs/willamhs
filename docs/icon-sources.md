# Icon sources and attribution

Original branding, banners, area icons, separators, buttons and charts were designed specifically for **Willam | Data & AI**. Technology and social marks are upstream brand icons.

- **[Devicon v2.17.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.17.0)** — [MIT license](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/v2.17.0/LICENSE). Original vendor colors are preserved. Individual trademarks remain their owners' property.
- **[Simple Icons v11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0)** and **[v15.0.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/15.0.0)** — [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/15.0.0/LICENSE.md). Monochrome marks use catalog brand colors. Older versions retain marks that have since left the catalog.
- **[Seaborn v0.13.2](https://github.com/mwaskom/seaborn/tree/v0.13.2)** — official project logo, [BSD 3-Clause license](https://github.com/mwaskom/seaborn/blob/v0.13.2/LICENSE.md).
- **[XGBoost](https://xgboost.ai/)**, **[LightGBM v4.6.0](https://github.com/microsoft/LightGBM/tree/v4.6.0/docs/logo)** and **[CatBoost](https://catboost.ai/docs/en/)** — logos from the official projects. CatBoost's white mark uses a navy tile to preserve the published colors. XGBoost and CatBoost's published website assets are tracked by their source digest rather than a versioned URL.

The exact upstream URL and SHA-256 digest of every downloaded source are in [sources.json](../assets/icons/sources.json). Icons are validated as static XML before use. Upstream vectors are nested in a neutral rounded tile to remain legible in light and dark modes. The digest describes the source bytes, before adding the tile and brand color.

SQL and the architectural concepts LLMs, RAG, AI Agents, Vector Databases, ETL, ELT and Data Warehousing use text: they do not have a single official technology logo. Vertex AI uses the Google Cloud parent mark and Looker Studio uses the Looker mark, explicitly identified in the README. These are not represented as standalone product logos.

No mark implies certification, endorsement, affiliation or measured proficiency.
